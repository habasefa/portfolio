# about

Building with LLMs, shipping to real users

I'm a Full-Stack AI Engineer based in Addis Ababa, building AI products from RAG, agents, and retrieval through mobile UX, backend, and the cloud infrastructure that keeps them running.

I founded two education startups. PrepX is an AI tutoring app (1,000+ installs, 250 MAU, 5★) with textbook-grounded retrieval over ~229K curriculum questions, kept model-agnostic via OpenRouter. Temaribet is a tutoring marketplace I started as a freshman and scaled to 4,000+ tutors and 500+ families — selected into Reach for Change and Orbit Innovation Hub.

Alongside founding, I ship production software for US and EU startups (React Native, NestJS / FastAPI, vector + full-text search, AWS + Nginx) and maintain react-native-ajora, an open-source agent-UI library with 6,100+ npm downloads.
